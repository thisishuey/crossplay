### What is new in 1.14.1-fork1790145182

- SUDOKU+, a cell-first Sudoku next to the original, with pencil notes as dots, row, column and box shading, and a MENU for hints and check.
- Hearts, the shelf's first trick-taking game.
- Live: leave a handwritten note on a reader asleep on somebody's fridge.
- Notes: lists you tick with one hand, and a deck of cards.
- Calculator, with arithmetic that is actually decimal.
- A Library on Home: search the card by title or author, or browse by folder.
- The Home button takes its own tap, double-tap and long-press actions.
- Rename files and bookmarks on the device, set the time by timezone, and an About screen in Settings.
- Reading is lighter on memory, plus WiFi, list-numbering, JPEG and frontlight fixes.
